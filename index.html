About Me

Beyond the Canvas

I'm a UI/UX designer expanding into full-stack development, applied AI, and motion graphics, driven by the belief that exceptional digital products are created when design, motion, and code speak the same language.

Instead of handing off static mockups with guesswork, I build real, working experiences. I combine rigorous UX research, design systems, motion design, and modern web technologies with intelligent LLM agent workflows to transform complex technical capabilities into intuitive, expressive, and delightful user interactions.

From interface design and interaction systems to motion graphics and functional prototypes, I enjoy turning ideas into experiences that don't just look good, but communicate, respond, and work.
